[[small-mercies]]
=== First steps with Small Mercies

Small Mercies is JVMud's bundled toy mudlib: five rooms, a few talkative NPCs,
and sparring opponents with more confidence than good sense. It demonstrates a
native LPC profile without database setup, account registration, or saved characters.
Use it to learn the player flow and to find small source examples.

==== Launch and create a guest

From the repository root:

[source,shell]
----
scripts/jvmud-start mudlibs/smallmercies/jvmud/smallmercies.config
----

Wait for `JVMud mudlib listening on 127.0.0.1:4000`. In a second terminal or
Telnet-capable MUD client, connect to `127.0.0.1` on port `4000`:

[source,shell]
----
telnet 127.0.0.1 4000
----

Answer each prompt separately:

. Choose a name containing 2–16 letters. It must be different from the names of
  players currently connected.
. Choose `male` or `female`.
. Choose `warrior` or `mage`.

You arrive in the *Village Square*. Type `help`, then `score`.
There is no password. A guest character exists for one connection: disconnecting
ends that character's visit, and reconnecting starts character creation again.

[cols="1,1,1,1,1",options="header,unbreakable"]
|===
|Class |STR |INT |DEX |Starting HP
|Warrior |12 |6 |9 |32
|Mage |6 |12 |9 |26
|===

Warriors use STR for their attacks; mages use INT for sparks. DEX helps dodge.
`score` also shows victories and the current opponent, when fighting. Gender
changes the character description, not the attributes or rules.

==== Explore the whole world

Every exit has a return route. Read `look` to see a description, exits, and nearby
characters. Use full directions, their initials, or `go <direction>`.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Place |Exits
|Village Square |`west` to The Resting Hero; `north` to the Municipal Herb Garden;
`east` to Very Short Lane.
|The Resting Hero |`east` to the square.
|Municipal Herb Garden |`south` to the square.
|Very Short Lane |`west` to the square; `down` to the Cellar of Mild Peril.
|Cellar of Mild Peril |`up` to the lane.
|===

From a fresh arrival in the square, enter these player commands one at a time:

[source,text]
----
look
talk mayor
west
talk innkeeper
rest
east
north
look goose
south
east
down
look rat
up
west
----

You finish back in the square. `rest` restores full health only at the inn.
`talk` addresses an NPC in your current Place. Try `who` to list connected
adventurers. `say Hello!` and `smile`, `wave`, `bow`, or `laugh` provide local
communication. Connect a second guest to try these with another player.

==== Try a short sparring match

From the square, go `north`, then type `attack goose`. Combat repeats every two
seconds; you do not need to repeat the attack command. `kill goose` is an alias.
Use `stop` or move `south` to end the bout. The rat in the cellar is the other
sparring opponent; the mayor and innkeeper are friendly.

A defeated NPC rests for 20 seconds before recovering. A player defeat sends you
to the inn with 1 HP; `rest` restores full health. NPCs spar with one challenger
at a time; their remaining HP persists when a visitor stops or leaves. There is no player-versus-player combat. If an opponent is resting or
occupied, return later rather than repeatedly issuing `attack`.

Use `score` to check health and victories, then `quit` to end your visit.
`//quit` is the default transport disconnect control. Neither creates a saved
character. NPC state remains shared while the server runs; restarting resets the
world. Stop the server with Ctrl+C in its terminal when finished.

==== Read the implementation

The manifest selects `room/square` and `player/adventurer`, maps object loading to
`create`, interaction scope to `init`, session connection to `connect`, and
session disconnection to `disconnect`. It grants `session_control` and maps
recurring ticks to `tick`; there is no filesystem-account policy or database.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
|Path under `mudlibs/smallmercies/` |What to learn
|`jvmud/smallmercies.config` |Explicit source root, preloads, Persona, initial
Place, lifecycle mappings, and capability grant.
|`source/lib/room.c` |Shared descriptions, authored exit lookup, and NPC creation.
|`source/room/*.c` |Small Place definitions with prose, exits, and resident NPCs.
|`source/player/adventurer.c` |Guest creation, command registration, movement,
communication, score, and recurring combat.
|`source/npc/villager.c` |NPC identity, dialogue, single-challenger combat, and
deferred recovery.
|===

These LPC examples use JVMud-native efuns. Game rules stay in the mudlib; the
host supplies execution, session, location, and scheduling services. Read the
source before changing behavior, then replay the relevant route after restarting.
Use a separate copy for experiments you do not intend to keep in the distribution.

The two-guest live Telnet check lives at
`src/test/scripts/smallmercies/smoke-play.py`.
