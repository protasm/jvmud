=== Terms used in procedures

[[_terminology]]
A *Player* is an identity; a *Session* is a connection; a *Persona* is the Entity
through which that Player acts in a World. A *Place* provides locality, a *Link*
connects Places, and a *Location* identifies containment. A loaded LPC object
can also be a service with no physical presence.

A *manifest* is the profile's `.config` file. It selects source paths, startup
objects, lifecycle mappings, language features, and engine capabilities. A
*profile* includes this manifest and the mudlib-side adapters needed to host that
content. Legacy terms such as `room` and `heart_beat` are used when naming actual
LPC paths or compatibility methods.
