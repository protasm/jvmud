Engine principles

Philosophy

JVMud is an opinionated engine for persistent, multiplayer, text-based game worlds in the LP tradition.

Core Concepts

The engine is organized around game, text, interactive play, multiplayer, world, persistence, temporality, and presence. Those ideas shape the runtime more than legacy driver vocabulary does.

Engine And Mudlib

JVMud treats LPC compatibility as its chosen target, while keeping the engine model clean. Legacy names can exist in compatibility shims; the engine itself should speak in terms of world reality.
